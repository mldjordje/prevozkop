# SEO Content Brief Template (City Landing)

## 1) Primary target
- Service: `behaton` or `beton`
- City slug: `{city-slug}`
- Main keyword: `behaton {city}` or `beton {city}`

## 2) Required sections
1. Hero with city + main offer.
2. Why local logistics matters in this city.
3. Typical use cases/projects in this city.
4. Process (request -> logistics -> delivery/installation).
5. FAQ (3-5 city-specific questions).
6. CTA block with call + form.

## 3) On-page SEO
- Unique `title` and `description` (no duplicates).
- Canonical: exact city URL.
- Internal links:
  - to main service page,
  - to related product pages,
  - to contact/order page.
- Add `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.

## 4) Conversion data
- Default form subject: `{service} {city} - upit`
- Track source page and UTM params.
- Include local trust points (response time, served zones, logistics).
